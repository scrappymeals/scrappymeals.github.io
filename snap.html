Operating Model

How I would operate inside a brand design team.

The role calls for a senior IC who can make work, lead through taste, explain the why, and scale the system. This is the practical model: no fog machine, no pointless ritual sacrifice to the gods of process.

01 · Frame
Clarify the brief and strongest visual path

Define audience, message, channel, format, stakeholder pressure, and the decision the design needs to make easier.

02 · Explore
Develop visual territories fast

Use sketches, Figma boards, AI exploration, motion references, layout studies, and mood logic to find the right direction quickly.

03 · Systemize
Turn the concept into reusable rules

Build templates, components, type rules, color behavior, diagrams, icon logic, export specs, and documentation that teams can use.

04 · Present
Explain tradeoffs and recommendations

Walk stakeholders through the decision-making, not just the comps. Reduce feedback chaos before it metastasizes.

05 · Ship
Protect craft through handoff

Stay close to specs, annotations, files, partner execution, accessibility, layout quality, and final polish across channels.
