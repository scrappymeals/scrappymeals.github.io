Section 04

30 · 60 · 90 Day Plan

Days 1-30

Understand + Frame

  • Learn Work & Co project context, client goals, product constraints, team operating style, and success metrics.
  • Audit existing product flows, design systems, research signals, stakeholder expectations, and technical dependencies.
  • Identify the core user problem and translate it into clear design priorities.
  • Build fast alignment artifacts: journey maps, flow diagrams, product principles, and early concept directions.
Days 31-60

Prototype + Validate

  • Lead iterative product design across UX, UI, visual direction, prototype behavior, and content hierarchy.
  • Partner closely with PMs, strategists, and developers to test feasibility and sharpen execution.
  • Prepare clickable prototypes and testable design directions for user feedback and client review.
  • Refine based on testing, stakeholder signal, technical reality, and product value.
Days 61-90

Launch + Optimize

  • Move strongest design direction into production-ready systems, specs, components, and QA standards.
  • Support developers through implementation details, edge cases, motion, responsive behavior, and visual polish.
  • Track launch performance and user feedback to identify optimization opportunities.
  • Document patterns and learnings so the team can move faster on the next cycle.

Direct Pitch

To the Work & Co team,

I’m interested in the Senior Design Lead role because it sits exactly where I do my strongest work: digital product strategy, visual design, interaction logic, prototyping, storytelling, technical collaboration, and hands-on execution from concept through launch.

My background is not a traditional single-lane product design path, and that is the advantage. I have worked across real-time 3D systems, live broadcast graphics, AI product workflows, founder-led product building, immersive brand worlds, pitch systems, and client-facing creative technology. The through-line is always the same: take something complex, make it understandable, design the path, prototype the experience, and help ship it with craft.

For Work & Co, I would bring a senior design partner who can work directly with PMs, strategists, developers, partners, and clients without needing layers of translation. I can help shape user flows, visual systems, prototypes, product narratives, launch details, and optimization cycles while keeping the end-user and product value in focus.

I care about the thing actually working. Strategy should become a flow. A flow should become a prototype. A prototype should reveal what users understand. A design system should help the product scale. And the final execution should feel sharp enough that nobody has to explain why it matters with a 67-slide hostage situation.
