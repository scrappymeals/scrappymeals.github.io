05 · Synopsis

A food-rescue world built to move kids from watching to doing.

Fridgie x Scraps* is a short-form animated series in which Fridgie and Scraps* turn overlooked ingredients into playful rescue adventures. Each episode makes food discovery feel like a story kids want to enter, not a lesson they are being asked to complete.

Story → Play → Impact

Entertainment with purpose.

The food lesson lives inside the adventure, so curiosity comes first and learning happens naturally.

The story continues in the app.

FridgieSmalls.app turns an episode’s idea into a simple real-world Rescue Mission using food already at home.

A universe designed to grow.

Characters, episodes, recipes, games, toys, classrooms, partnerships and immersive experiences can all extend from the same recognizable world.

What makes it unique

It is not only a cartoon, a recipe app or a food-waste campaign. It is one connected children’s entertainment and food-tech system that can build affection for the characters, useful habits at home and a scalable original property at the same time.
