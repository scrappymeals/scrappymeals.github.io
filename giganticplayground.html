Connected Process
How I work.
01

Discovery & Concept

Start with the audience, desired feeling or behavior, business goal, and possibilities of the medium.

02

Prototype & Test

Move the idea quickly into something people can see, touch, test, and react to.

03

Production & Build

Bring creative and engineering together early enough that technology expands the idea rather than constraining it.

04

Launch & Iterate

Stay close through launch, watch how the experience behaves in the real world, and improve it.
