A note to the Notion team

Why this feels
like a natural next page.

I’m drawn to products that give people a clear starting point—and the freedom to build something of their own.

Make powerful things approachable

At Fridgie Smalls, I translated ingredients, preferences, and AI reasoning into a simple question: “What can I make with what I have?” That same instinct draws me to flexible tools with a clear mental model.

Design the system, not just the screen

From reusable product states to graphics systems used by entire production teams, my work connects individual interactions to a coherent whole. Patterns have to hold up when people use them in unexpected ways.

Close the gap between idea and shipped

I work across Figma, React, TypeScript, APIs, and production QA. I can explore an interaction, prototype the hard part, and partner with engineering with a clear understanding of the tradeoffs.

Treat AI as a product experience

Context, structured inputs, fallback states, speed, and cost all shape whether an AI feature is useful. I’ve owned those decisions in a consumer product, including a 72% reduction in input-token usage.

Less friction. More room to think.
