Section 06

30 / 60 / 90 Product Plan

Marketplace & Commerce: from fragmented journey to measurable growth system. The plan starts with visibility, moves to focused experiments, and ends with an operating model Unity can keep using.

First 30 Days

Map the journey and expose the gaps

  • Audit the end-to-end user path from marketing touchpoints to Unity web, commerce, marketplace discovery, account creation, asset usage, product activation, retention, and churn.
  • Interview stakeholders across Marketplace, Commerce, Marketing, Web, Product, Growth, Data Science, Engineering, and Sales to understand goals, ownership, blockers, and current metrics.
  • Create a shared funnel map that identifies conversion points, drop-offs, duplicate work, unclear ownership, and missing feedback loops.
  • Review current initiatives such as Magenta commerce, user groups, asset distribution, AI GTM, and marketplace usage patterns.
  • Define the first version of a metrics framework: acquisition source, conversion, asset discovery, purchase intent, product activation, usage depth, retention, churn, and expansion indicators.
Days 31 - 60

Prioritize opportunities and launch focused experiments

  • Translate the funnel audit into a prioritized opportunity backlog with business cases, effort estimates, risk, owners, and expected impact.
  • Identify two to three high-leverage experiments around marketplace discovery, commerce conversion, asset usage, user group expansion, or lifecycle engagement.
  • Partner with data science to form hypotheses around recommendation signals, search behavior, asset relevance, creator segments, and churn patterns.
  • Work with marketing and web teams to test clearer messaging paths from top-of-funnel campaigns into product and marketplace actions.
  • Define AI-assisted GTM opportunities, including personalized user journeys, smarter asset discovery, creator education, and automated lifecycle triggers.
Days 61 - 90

Ship, measure, and build the operating model

  • Launch initial product or journey improvements tied to measurable funnel outcomes rather than subjective internal preferences.
  • Create a cross-functional Marketplace & Commerce operating rhythm with roadmap reviews, experiment readouts, decision logs, metric updates, and clear escalation paths.
  • Deliver a six-month roadmap covering funnel optimization, commerce improvements, asset distribution, AI GTM use cases, and retention opportunities.
  • Establish a simple executive dashboard that shows what is improving, what is blocked, what is being tested, and where investment should go next.
  • Turn early wins into repeatable product guidance so Unity has a durable system for discovering and acting on growth opportunities across the ecosystem.
