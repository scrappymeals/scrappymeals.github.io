Why this car matters

The value proposition is not simply “an early Mustang.” It is the intersection of launch-period production, desirable D-code performance specification, convertible body style, 4-speed transmission, uncommon color, low mileage, limited ownership, and preservation. If independent inspection confirms the originality claims, this car belongs in a materially different category from a typical restored 1965 Mustang convertible.

Why survivor rarity may be much stronger

The model above does not yet filter for blue interior, April 23 launch-period scheduling, ~49,000 miles, two-owner provenance, original drivetrain, original sheet metal, original factory components, or unrestored preservation. Those variables could reduce the number of genuinely comparable surviving cars dramatically.
