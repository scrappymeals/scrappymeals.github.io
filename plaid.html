06 / Process

How the system scales

A simple production loop built for a growing social function.

01

Brief

Clarify objective, audience, channel, urgency, source material, and the actual behavior the post should drive.

02

Concept

Find the social-native idea first, then decide what format best expresses it instead of starting with an asset size.

03

Design

Build in Figma and Adobe with strong type, composition, visual rhythm, motion logic, and a clear brand signature.

04

Adapt

Translate the idea for LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Reddit, paid, OOH, or launch support without flattening every platform into one voice.

05

Review

Get fast feedback from social and brand partners, resolve the important craft issues, and keep the work moving.

06

Systemize

Capture what works as components, references, recurring series, motion rules, and briefing standards that increase speed over time.
