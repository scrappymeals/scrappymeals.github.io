First 90 Days at Meta

The goal is not to enter Meta and perform theater. It is to learn the system, identify high-signal product opportunities, build trust with engineering/design/research/data, and turn ambiguity into execution.

Days 1-30

Learn the System

  • Map the product area: users, business goals, technical architecture, org structure, roadmap, constraints, success metrics, and decision rituals.
  • Meet engineering, design, research, data science, policy, legal, marketing, and operations partners to understand what is actually blocking progress.
  • Audit current product surfaces, user feedback, experiments, metrics, quality issues, and AI workflow opportunities.
Days 31-60

Define High-Signal Bets

  • Frame product hypotheses tied to user value, platform strategy, measurable outcomes, technical feasibility, and team capacity.
  • Build crisp product briefs, requirements, success metrics, risk notes, and stakeholder narratives.
  • Identify where AI can reduce workflow friction, improve quality, personalize experiences, or accelerate learning without compromising trust.
Days 61-90

Execute and Scale

  • Drive one or two focused execution tracks with engineering, design, research, and data partners.
  • Set clear goals, launch criteria, experiment design, review cadence, and decision thresholds.
  • Package the operating model into reusable team rituals, metrics dashboards, roadmap clarity, and future product opportunities.
