Section 02 · Wearables Design Model

Designing for Captured Perspective

Wearables are not just smaller screens. They are capture devices, social signals, context machines, and trust surfaces. The design challenge is to make new perspectives feel useful, quick, controlled, and shareable without making users feel like they are operating a cockpit strapped to their face.

Instagram Wearables Interaction Layer

01 Capture from glasses. Curate on mobile. Share through Instagram with confidence, context, and control.
02 Design for speed, privacy cues, lightweight review, smart suggestions, and social-native storytelling.
03 Prototype the moment, not just the screen. Device behavior, mobile UI, AI assist, and share flow must feel connected.

Wearables Product Design Priorities

For Instagram across Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses, display glasses, and AR devices, Damon would treat mobile as the control surface and the wearable as the new perspective engine.

Capture MomentsDesign features around first-person perspective, spontaneous sharing, low-friction review, and content confidence.
Reduce Cognitive LoadSmall-screen and wearable interactions need fewer decisions, clearer hierarchy, and faster confirmation loops.
Prototype BehaviorUse high-fidelity prototypes and interactive models to communicate motion, states, device handoffs, and edge cases.
Build TrustPrivacy, control, accuracy, AI suggestions, and user agency must be designed into the experience, not stapled on later like compliance duct tape.
Align TeamsPartner across Instagram, Wearables, PM, engineering, research, and design leadership to make emerging-device ambiguity actionable.
Mobile as control surface · wearable as perspective engine · Instagram as social layer

  • Frames AI decisions around user agency, transparency, accuracy, bias mitigation, and privacy-aware interaction.
