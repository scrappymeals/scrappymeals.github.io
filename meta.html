First 90 Days

A focused plan for sales operations tooling, AI adoption, and maintainable architecture.

The goal is to learn the domain quickly, identify the leverage points, and ship visible improvements while building the architecture discipline needed for long-term ownership.

Days 1–30

Map the operating reality.

  • Interview retail, sales, operations, partnerships, program, engineering, analytics, and vendor stakeholders.
  • Inventory current tools, data flows, business records, handoffs, maintenance gaps, and AI opportunities.
  • Document pain points by business impact: productivity loss, decision latency, data quality, manual effort, and support burden.
  • Identify near-term wins that do not compromise security, privacy, maintainability, or roadmap coherence.
Days 31–60

Define the technical roadmap.

  • Translate priority problems into solution briefs, architecture diagrams, data models, and implementation options.
  • Partner with engineering, data analytics, data engineering, business teams, and vendors to define build-versus-integrate paths.
  • Create AI workflow patterns with context design, model behavior controls, accuracy reviews, and responsible-use criteria.
  • Set documentation, monitoring, support, continuity, and release-readiness standards for owned solutions.
Days 61–90

Ship, measure, and scale.

  • Deliver the first improvement initiative or prototype tied to a clear productivity, efficiency, or insight-generation metric.
  • Publish operational playbooks so business-facing teams can adopt the workflow and support teams can maintain it.
  • Review quality, accuracy, user behavior, and adoption data; refine roadmap priorities based on measurable signal.
  • Establish a repeatable intake-to-launch model for future technical programs.
