Product Proof

Fridgie Smalls: a consumer product case study in utility, delight, iteration, and economics.

The user problem

People often have ingredients at home but no plan. Fridgie reduces the friction between inventory and action: users speak or type what they have, receive personalized recipe options, save useful ideas, build grocery decisions, and return for new combinations.

  • Voice-first mobile UX:  instant recording, animated prompts, clear stop/send state, and typing fallback for kitchen-context use.
  • Responsive product craft:  full-screen recipe cards, pinned actions, independent scrolling, title auto-scaling, and orientation-aware phone/tablet behavior.
  • Experimentation:  iterated AI behavior, Recipe Remix, interface states, branded intros, content generation, and user workflows.
  • Business impact:  reduced prompt weight and response cost while improving speed and maintaining a more engaging product experience.
  • Monetization strategy:  used user behavior, research, product performance, and cost data to guide a pivot balancing acquisition, engagement, retention, affordability, sponsorship, and long-term growth.

Product Design Process

Loose concept → clear behavior → tested, polished product.

I move between strategy and tactical craft deliberately. The process is lightweight enough for fast-moving product teams but structured enough to create rationale, feedback loops, and quality.

01

Understand

Clarify user behavior, business value, constraints, context, current friction, and the evidence that should change the decision.

02

Frame

Reduce ambiguity into a clear product problem, success signal, priority, user flow, and set of decisions the team can act on.

03

Prototype

Use Figma, high-fidelity interaction work, motion, content, and code-informed prototypes to make the experience discussable early.

04

Partner

Work with Product and Engineering on tradeoffs, technical states, edge cases, scope, requirements, and what belongs now versus later.

05

Experiment

Test interaction behavior, content, performance, cost, state design, feedback, and product assumptions; document what changed and why.

06

Refine

Agonize over the last mile: copy, animation, hierarchy, responsiveness, error recovery, consistency, QA, business impact, and the next iteration.
