Why Profound

The role is the overlap.

I am not trying to look like a conventional web designer. The value is the overlap of visual craft, product thinking, AI, implementation awareness and production execution.

01 / Design with constraints

I design with the real system in mind.

Responsive UI, component logic, APIs, browser behavior, performance, QA and deployment are not handoff concerns. They shape the design from the beginning.

02 / AI as product

I have shipped AI, not just explored it.

Fridgie Smalls uses LLM workflows inside an actual consumer product. That means prompt behavior, cost, latency, fallback states, user input, feature design and continuous production iteration.

03 / High ambiguity

Zero-to-one is familiar territory.

My career repeatedly starts with “we need this to exist” rather than a finished brief. I define the system, make the prototype tangible and then work through what it takes to make it real.

04 / Finish line

I stay through launch.

Years of live and interactive production trained me to care about the last 10%: browser testing, edge cases, asset behavior, data, implementation details and whether the thing works when people actually use it.

One-line version

Profound needs someone who can make ambitious AI products feel inevitable.

That is the work I want: taking a technically complex product, finding the clearest expression of it, and pushing the experience until the design and the implementation feel like one thing.
