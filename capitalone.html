First 90 Days

A practical AI-in-XD roadmap for fast learning, safe experimentation, and executive alignment.

The plan is written for a top-of-house XD role: build trust quickly, make the AI opportunity concrete, define the governance model, and give teams practical patterns they can use immediately.

Days 1–30

Listen, map, and establish the transformation frame.

  • Meet with the CDO, Design SLT, Product, Engineering, Risk, Legal, Data, Research, and design-system leaders.
  • Inventory current AI experimentation, tooling access, pain points, duplication, policy constraints, and workflow bottlenecks.
  • Define adoption principles: human-centered, measurable, secure, explainable, accessible, and useful to designers day-to-day.
  • Create the initial AI-in-XD opportunity map across research synthesis, journey mapping, prototyping, content workflows, design QA, and handoff.
Days 31–60

Pilot the highest-value workflows with executive-visible rigor.

  • Select 2–3 high-value pilots across product design, service design, content design, or design-system acceleration.
  • Evaluate tools and workflows such as enterprise-approved AI assistants, assistive IDEs, prototyping accelerators, state-model methods, and structured prompt libraries.
  • Define guardrails: data handling, human review, quality thresholds, inclusive language, accessibility, and research/data interpretation standards.
  • Publish pilot scorecards tied to cycle time, quality, adoption, risk reduction, customer value, and business value.
Days 61–90

Scale what works into the design operating model.

  • Convert successful pilots into repeatable playbooks, templates, design-system extensions, model-use patterns, and training modules.
  • Build an AI design practice roadmap with quarterly milestones, operating cadences, ownership, adoption metrics, and executive decision points.
  • Launch role-based upskilling paths for designers, managers, design technologists, researchers, content designers, and cross-functional partners.
  • Align Design SLT on the next horizon: durable AI tooling roadmap, governance structure, and measurable contribution to product outcomes.
