How I work

Issue. Impact. Resolution.

Issue

Frame the actual problem

Separate symptoms from root causes using customer feedback, product context, business realities, operational data, and technical constraints.

Impact

Make tradeoffs visible

Clarify who is affected, what the business consequence is, and which dependencies or constraints shape the solution space.

Resolution

Move from insight to execution

Align stakeholders around the strongest path, prototype where useful, define quality, and stay involved through implementation and QA.

First 90 days

Learn deeply, align deliberately, then raise the bar.

The first objective is not to arrive with a pre-written answer. It is to understand the portfolio, customers, team, design system, business goals, research, and constraints well enough to make better decisions.

Days 1 to 30

Understand

  • Learn the product portfolio, customer journeys, business goals, and major experience pain points.
  • Understand the design team, operating model, research practices, and critique culture.
  • Review the established design system and implementation patterns.
  • Build relationships across Product, Tech, Research, and Business.
Days 31 to 60

Align

  • Identify the highest-value customer and business problems.
  • Clarify journey maps, service blueprints, dependencies, and opportunity areas.
  • Strengthen critique, decision-making, and cross-functional communication where needed.
  • Establish shared success measures with partner teams.
Days 61 to 90

Advance

  • Move priority opportunities into concepts, prototypes, and high-fidelity direction.
  • Define measurable product and design outcomes.
  • Support individual growth and team development plans.
  • Create alignment around the next phase of portfolio experience strategy.
